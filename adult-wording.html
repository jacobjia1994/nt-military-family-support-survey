Survey of Service Member and Family Needs

Greater Darwin

Welcome to the Survey of Service Member and Family Needs

Lutheran Care — Greater Darwin

This adult questionnaire is for people aged 18 years or older who are current or former Australian Defence Force members, or members of their families, with a connection to Greater Darwin.

Military personnel from other countries serving in Greater Darwin and their adult family members are also welcome. Family members may live elsewhere.

Service Member and Family Needs

Purpose of the Survey

Lutheran Care wants to learn more about the needs of service members and their families and how well those needs are being met.

Lutheran Care is preparing to deliver a Defence family support program in Greater Darwin. Before activities begin, we are listening to service members and families. The consultation is intended to inform how planned family supports are developed and delivered, and to identify where other help may be needed.

The project is funded by the Australian Government Department of Defence through its Family Support Funding Program.

Who Is Being Asked to Take the Survey

This adult questionnaire is for current or former ADF members and their family members aged 18 years or older who are connected with Greater Darwin. It also includes military personnel from other countries serving in Greater Darwin and their adult family members.

Family members include spouses, partners, former partners, parents, adult children, other relatives, kin and carers. Taking part is not an application for a service or activity.

What Survey Participation Involves

Participation involves completing this web-based survey.

The survey will ask you about different kinds of problems you may have encountered in the past year, what kinds of assistance you may have needed to help you with those problems, and whether you were satisfied with the kinds of support available to you for those problems.

In the last sections, we will ask some background questions and you will have the opportunity to comment on the survey itself or on family issues that the survey did not cover.

Personal Information

Please do not enter names, contact details, service numbers or details that identify other people. Do not include classified or operationally sensitive information.

Participation Is Entirely Voluntary

Your participation in this survey is voluntary. You may decide not to participate now or at any time. If you feel uncomfortable answering any of the questions, you may skip to the next question. You may stop taking the survey at any time.

Whom to Contact

For questions about the consultation, contact Lutheran Care’s Darwin Office.
