Defence Family Support Survey

Hello, Defence community!

Lutheran Care would like your help to plan its Defence Family Support Program in Greater Darwin.

You can share a family experience, something that has worked well, or an idea for the coming months.

Please answer about your own experience.

Lutheran Care received funding from Defence Member and Family Support, a branch of the Commonwealth Department of Defence, to deliver this project.
