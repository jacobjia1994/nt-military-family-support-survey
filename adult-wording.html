Defence family support survey

Lutheran Care would like your help to plan its Defence family support program in the Northern Territory.

This questionnaire asks about your own experience: the support you have needed, what you have received and what would help now. We will use your answers to decide what the local program should focus on and how we offer it.

You are welcome to take part whether you need extra support or things are going well. Family members living outside the NT are welcome too. Other adults in your family can answer separately.

Thank you for your time.

The program is funded by the Australian Government Department of Defence through its Family Support Funding Program.

Taking part and your information

Taking part Taking part is your choice. Most questions are optional, and you can stop before submitting. Some ask about wellbeing, disability or safety. Please answer about your own experience and leave out names, service numbers, units and other identifying details.

Why we are asking Lutheran Care will use your answers to plan its Defence family support program in the Northern Territory. You can take part if you live outside the NT. We are interested in your experience while you or someone in your family is serving here.

Keeping your information Lutheran Care will hold your answers in its approved record-keeping systems. Access is limited to authorised people who need the information for this work. Records are kept and securely disposed of under Lutheran Care’s records requirements.

Sharing and confidentiality Lutheran Care will share project information with the Department of Defence in a form that does not reasonably identify participants. We may need to disclose information when required by law or to protect someone from serious harm, including concerns about a child’s safety.

Changing your mind You can ask to withdraw your answers after submitting. If we cannot identify which answers are yours, we may be unable to remove them.

Privacy and questions You can ask to access or correct your personal information, or raise a privacy concern, at feedback@lutherancare.org.au. Our Privacy Policy explains how Lutheran Care handles personal information and complaints.

Getting help This questionnaire helps plan services. It does not arrange support, a referral or a follow-up interview. In Australia, call 000 in an emergency. For domestic, family or sexual violence support, contact 1800RESPECT on 1800 737 732.

Your agreement

I have read the participant information and agree to take part. I consent to Lutheran Care collecting, using and sharing my answers as described, including any health or disability information I choose to provide about myself.
