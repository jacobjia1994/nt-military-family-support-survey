# Greater Darwin Defence Family Support Survey — current product contract

Updated 30 September 2026. One adult open-response questionnaire is published at `index.html`: exactly four pages — welcome/informed agreement, A bit about you, Your living experiences, and Thank you. Choice and non-adult questionnaire routes are removed. The earlier two-variant and RAND reconstructions are recoverable through Git; they do not govern this entry.

The audience remains currently serving personnel based in Greater Darwin and their adult family members, including overseas military families and family members living elsewhere. All background and narrative answers are optional; adult participation agreement is required. Six supplied substantive prompts and one final comment use independent raw-text answers, each limited to 5,000 characters for completion. No count is shown and pasted text is preserved if over the limit.

Location prioritises Darwin, Palmerston and Litchfield; Other area reveals two secondary locations. Optional locality remains available. Prefer-not answers are omitted; a selected radio answer can be cleared. Dependants use an optional Yes/No question and two conditional counts, without age bands. NT residence duration is two optional whole numbers: years 0–99 and months 0–11. See [ADULT_SURVEY.md](ADULT_SURVEY.md) for the field and wording decisions.

The welcome uses a factual invitation and eligibility paragraph, then a grouped two-column information panel with integrated adult/sensitive-information agreement. Programme contact and de-identified reporting use are adapted from LC’s supplied previous Forms survey. Source and funding credits sit after Start survey. Thank you offers two equal action areas for the independent interview request and NT service finder. These links carry no answers or identifying state. No Leave survey button is displayed.

This remains a static presentation with no answer receiver, answer persistence or analytics. Completion is neutral and never claims receipt. Actual collection needs a receiving arrangement and the project-specific information-handling facts recorded in ADULT_SURVEY.md; this revision does not activate collection.
