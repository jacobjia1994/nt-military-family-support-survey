## Free support finder

On 25 September 2026 Jacob asked for a standalone resource that lets NT Defence members and families find relevant services in a few clicks. `support.html` is separate from the questionnaire and interview form; it does not need either form to be completed and carries no answers or contact details to a service link.

Jacob rejected the first six-area navigation because overlapping life situations forced families to guess an exclusive category. The accepted replacement starts with recognisable situations in `support-journeys.mjs`. A clear situation opens a short curated service list directly; a broad situation offers one set of specific choices before services. The 39 headings in `support-data.mjs` are internal evidence tags and can appear through multiple journeys. Age, place and eligibility are surfaced where they change which service is useful; language and privacy support remain available as contextual links. No general profile quiz is required. Feedback on service design is a secondary footer route, and a human-help journey serves people unsure where to turn. No topic search or generic instruction copy appears. Global urgent contacts are in the footer; immediate danger advice stays visible within relevant unsafe or acute-distress results.

Service cards show provider, purpose, locality, decisive limitation and a contact action. Full audience and access conditions open on request. Official provider records were checked on 25 September 2026; current availability and eligibility remain provider decisions. The finder does not collect or store choices.
