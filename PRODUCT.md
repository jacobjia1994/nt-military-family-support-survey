# Greater Darwin Defence Family Support Survey — current product contract

Lutheran Care is reviewing two adult questionnaire alternatives for the Greater Darwin Defence community. Both use the same opening, adult scope, basic questions, 20 issue categories with 170 specific issues, top-two priority-area selection, final comment and thanks. The team will review both before choosing one. [Detailed choices](index.html) is at the existing site entry; [open responses](open-response.html) has a separate entry; [compare](compare.html) is for the team. [SURVEY_VARIANTS.md](SURVEY_VARIANTS.md) records source-package hashes and the wording update path.

The choice-led version asks for selected kinds of help, sources, source characteristics and need outcomes. Each priority need has its own optional 10,000-character explanation. The open version asks written questions about the same functions, with no preset answer controls in the detailed middle. It keeps up to two independent need slots per priority area and never automatically codes written answers as choice responses. Both use no more than two detailed area pages within a five-to-seven-screen route.

Family members may live outside Greater Darwin while a currently serving family member is based here. The separate [youth/younger-child route](youth.html) retains its own participation path. Neither adult entry contains an answer receiver, analytics or browser answer persistence. Its visible wording follows the supplied real-survey text and ends with a neutral thank-you; completion is not a receipt. A future collection launch requires separate Lutheran Care decisions and an end-to-end receiving test.

The prior full RAND Appendix A Q1–Q67 adult reconstruction remains in the repository for provenance. It is not the current adult entry. [RAND_ITEM_CROSSWALK.md](RAND_ITEM_CROSSWALK.md) and `rand-adult-*.js` describe that historical build.
