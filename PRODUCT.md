# Greater Darwin Defence Family Support Survey — current product contract

Updated 30 September 2026. One adult open-response questionnaire is published at `index.html`: exactly four pages — welcome/informed agreement, A bit about you, Your living experiences, and Thank you. Choice and non-adult questionnaire routes are removed. The earlier two-variant and RAND reconstructions are recoverable through Git; they do not govern this entry.

The audience remains currently serving personnel based in Greater Darwin and their adult family members, including overseas military families and family members living elsewhere. All visible background and narrative questions require a response; selection questions provide Prefer not to answer and numeric groups have an explicit refusal. Adult participation agreement remains required. Six supplied substantive prompts and one final comment use independent raw-text answers, each limited to 5,000 characters for completion. No count is shown and pasted text is preserved if over the limit.

Location prioritises Darwin, Palmerston and Litchfield; Other area reveals two secondary locations. Visible locality must be answered or declined. A selected radio answer can be cleared, but the question must be answered before continuing. Dependants use Yes / No / Prefer not to answer and two conditional counts (or count refusal), without age bands. NT residence duration requires years 0–99 and months 0–11, or a duration refusal. See [ADULT_SURVEY.md](ADULT_SURVEY.md) for the field and wording decisions.

The welcome uses a factual invitation and eligibility paragraph, then a grouped two-column information panel with integrated adult/sensitive-information agreement. Programme contact and de-identified reporting use are adapted from LC’s supplied previous Forms survey. Source and funding credits sit after Start survey. Thank you offers two equal action areas for the independent interview request and NT service finder. These links carry no answers or identifying state. No Leave survey button is displayed.

This remains a static presentation with no answer receiver or analytics. Unfinished drafts are now persisted in the same browser for resumption, using a namespaced local-storage key and stable answer-schema version. Completion is neutral, never claims receipt and clears the draft. Actual collection needs a receiving arrangement and the project-specific information-handling facts recorded in ADULT_SURVEY.md; this revision does not activate collection.
