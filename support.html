Privacy, interpreters and using this page

Your choices stay on this page. No answers are submitted or connected to the survey. Closing or reloading the page clears them; your browser may still keep page history.

Ask the service to arrange an interpreter or accessible contact method. Before sharing personal details, you can ask what it records and who can see it.
