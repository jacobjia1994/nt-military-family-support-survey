# Interview request receiving proposal

27 September 2026 · A practical option for Lutheran Care (LC)

**If LC already has a suitable Microsoft 365 subscription, use an LC-owned Microsoft Forms group form to receive requests, a small separate contact log to track progress, and one coordinator with a backup to arrange private interviews.** Start with manual invitations offering two or three actual appointment options. This avoids building and maintaining a custom receiving service for a small consultation.

This proposal is ready for LC to consider alongside the [interview team guide](INTERVIEW_TEAM_GUIDE.md). No LC account, form, mailbox, automation or booking calendar has been created or changed. The current GitHub form remains a preview and does not transmit requests.

## Recommended first version

| Part | Proposed arrangement |
| --- | --- |
| Public entry | The existing **Request an interview** link opens LC’s hosted Microsoft Form. Participants enter their information once, there. |
| Ownership | Create the form within a small **private Microsoft 365 group from the outset**, with an ongoing LC lead and backup. Do not make a student’s personal or university account the sole owner. |
| External participation | Allow **Anyone can respond**, subject to LC’s existing tenant settings, so families do not need an LC account. Keep response access restricted; do not publish a results-summary link. |
| Contact details | Preserve Me / My child under 18. Ask self-requesters Under 18 / 18 or older; infer under-18 status on the guardian route without repeating the question. Require a genuine, contactable **mobile number on every route**. Professional contributors select Me. Email remains optional; require it only if the person chooses email to arrange a time. Supplying a mobile does not authorise a call when text or email was selected. |
| Receiving record | Use the Forms response view and, where useful, its linked group SharePoint workbook. Keep the original response record intact. |
| Working contact log | Maintain a **separate** small workbook in LC’s approved location: request reference, assigned coordinator, contact permissions, status, next action and confirmed appointment. Use the schema in the team guide; do not copy private stories into the log or link it to anonymous survey responses. |
| Scheduling | One coordinator offers two or three real slots and a way to say none works, using the chosen text/email/call channel. The participant’s reply and coordinator’s confirmation agree the booking; there is no compulsory preliminary interview or extra confirmation call. Send at most one unanswered-invitation reminder through the permitted channel, then close that invitation if unanswered. Follow the team guide for cancellation, confirmed appointments and waitlists. |

Microsoft supports creating forms within a Microsoft 365 group. Group members can manage the form; co-authors can view and edit both questions and responses. Therefore, use a deliberately limited private group, rather than an existing broad or public Team. Project participation does not automatically require access to every request. External respondents and external collaborators are separate permissions. [Group forms](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/forms/create-a-group-form-or-quiz), [collaboration access](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/forms/share-a-form-or-quiz-to-collaborate), [external response settings](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/forms/choose-who-can-fill-out-a-form-or-quiz), [administrator settings](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-forms/administrator-settings-microsoft-forms).

Keep workflow columns and tables out of the automatically synced response workbook. Microsoft warns that manually added content can cause sync errors, and moving a group form’s workbook can break its connection. The separate contact log avoids turning the receiving record into a fragile scheduling tool. A downloaded Excel copy is not a live inbox. [Workbook sync](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/forms/how-to-ensure-your-form-and-workbook-are-in-sync), [group response workbook](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/forms/move-your-form-to-a-group).

## Notifications and participant confirmation

Turn on **Get email notification of each response** for the receiving team, then test with invented details that the coordinator and backup receive the intended notification. Group email delivery also depends on the Outlook group arrangement. Notifications are prompts to check the receiving record; the coordinator’s agreed checking routine remains the way to prevent missed requests. [Forms notifications](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/forms/send-form-invitations-and-manage-notifications), [group notification guidance](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/forms/move-your-form-to-a-group).

After a real submission, the hosted form can show a short acknowledgement explaining that a request has been received but an interview is not yet booked, together with LC’s actual reply arrangements and the limited-capacity notice; it must not promise that everyone will be contacted. **Do not promise an automatic email receipt to external families:** Microsoft’s native email confirmation receipt is limited to respondents within the organisation when **Record Name** is enabled. A manually sent invitation through the person’s chosen channel is sufficient for the first version. Any later automatic email or SMS must respect contact permissions. [Response receipt limits](https://support.microsoft.com/en-au/forms/print-or-download-a-receipt-of-responses).

## How this would replace the preview

Recreate the current field contract in [CONTACT_FORM.md](CONTACT_FORM.md), then point the GitHub entry link to LC’s hosted form. Retain self/guardian and binary self-age routes, required mobile, optional email, scheduling-contact choices, voicemail permission, contact consent and the separate required capacity acknowledgement before submission. **Keep discussion topic, suggested interview date/time and interview mode as separate optional questions.** A single contact/access-needs field handles safe-contact and participation arrangements. Under-18 self-requesters can supply the same brief non-sensitive topic and scheduling preferences; LC establishes understanding, appropriate permission and willingness before the actual interview. Do not add a second guardian age question, online capacity assessment or ADF-connection/organisation-role questionnaire. Use invented adult, guardian and younger-person requests, including a professional using the ordinary self route, to check the experience before recruitment. Forms need not reproduce the custom page’s appearance exactly. Implement email scheduling as a branch that requests an email address; keep email optional for call/text and mobile required for every route. Do not assume native Forms reproduces the prototype’s cross-field checks or clears previous answers on every route change. Check these cases in the actual hosted form, and have the coordinator clarify inconsistent entries before making contact. Mobile instructions and an authorised reply establish usability; a required field alone cannot verify that a number is real.

**A Microsoft Forms link is not a receiving API for the existing JavaScript form.** Microsoft’s documented connector reads form/response details and triggers after a response is submitted; it does not list an action to submit an arbitrary custom-page response. The supported public collection routes are the hosted form link and official embed. On that evidence, a direct hosted link is the simplest maintainable option. Do not reverse-engineer a submission endpoint, put contact details in URL parameters, require people to fill out both forms, or treat opening a `mailto:` message as successful submission. [Forms connector](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/connectors/microsoftforms/), [link and embed options](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/forms/send-a-form-to-get-responses).

Keeping the current custom input interface for live collection would require a separately approved and maintained receiving service. That is a possible later design, but it adds work and is not necessary to run the proposed consultation.

## Add Bookings only when it earns its place

If LC already uses Microsoft Bookings and arranging times becomes repetitive, a **shared LC booking page** could let invited participants select from a limited set of private appointment slots. Bookings supports defined availability, calendar coordination and buffer time. Keep the manual route for contact restrictions, child participation or arrangements needing discussion; automatic reminders must fit the agreed contact channel. No new purchase or automation is proposed for the first version. [Bookings features and licensing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/office365/servicedescriptions/microsoft-bookings-service-description), [availability and buffers](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/bookings/configure-service-availability?view=o365-worldwide).

## Set-up and handover when LC adopts the proposal

LC can fill in the working arrangements already listed in the team guide: the actual receiver, authorised users, coordinator and backup, reply contact, checking routine, retention and handover. Its Microsoft 365 administrator can verify Forms availability, permitted external responses and the actual data location. **An Australian organisation does not by itself establish Australian Forms storage**; Microsoft’s current location guidance distinguishes tenants by when Forms was provisioned. Describe LC’s verified arrangement in the participant notice. [Forms data location](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/forms/data-storage-for-microsoft-forms).

Start with group ownership so the intake does not depend on a student remaining in placement. If a personal form is moved to the group, obtain and test its current group sharing link before use: Microsoft notes that the old personal link can stop working after the original owner’s account is permanently deleted. Before placement ends, the LC lead accepts the open-request log and upcoming appointments, and access is updated through LC’s normal process. [Ownership and link behaviour](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/forms/move-your-form-to-a-group), [administrator transfer guidance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-forms/admin-information).

These are implementation details for LC’s chosen receiving arrangement, not reasons to delay reviewing the form or team workflow now. If LC uses another approved platform, apply the same ownership, restricted access, safe contact and finite scheduling pattern there.
