About your answers

Your answers stay in this page while it is open. They are not sent to a server or added to the results example.

Refreshing or closing the page clears your answers. At the end, you can choose to download a copy. Avoid including names, contact details, units, schools or other identifying details in written answers.

You can skip questions, go back to change answers, or stop at any time. This questionnaire is not monitored for requests for help.
