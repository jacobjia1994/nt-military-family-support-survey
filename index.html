About your answers

Taking part is voluntary. Most questions are optional, and you can stop at any time.

Lutheran Care will use your answers to help plan support for Defence members and families. Project information will also be shared with the Department of Defence after identifying details have been removed.

Please leave out names, service numbers and other details that could identify you or someone else.

Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information, when it may be shared, and how to request access, correct information or make a complaint.

For privacy questions, contact feedback@lutherancare.org.au.
