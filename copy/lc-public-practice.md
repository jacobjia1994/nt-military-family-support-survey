# Lutheran Care public forms: verified practice for the Defence family consultation

Checked 24 September 2026. This is Lutheran Care (South Australia and Northern Territory), lutherancare.org.au. Public pages and forms were read without submitting anything.

## Finding

Use LC's existing confidentiality, records and complaint arrangements, expressed briefly for this consultation. Do not invent a separate privacy regime. A current public complaints form and two current referral forms were inspected in full. No publicly accessible Defence-family survey or comparable community-consultation instrument was located in this bounded search. A news article confirms a late-2025 Barossa community survey existed, but does not publish its questionnaire or collection notice.

## Short formal participant notice suitable for adaptation

This text is an adaptation of verified LC policy, with the project purpose and Defence sharing supplied by this project's brief/meeting record. It is not represented as a verbatim existing LC survey notice.

**About your answers**

Lutheran Care will use your answers to plan its Defence family support program in the Northern Territory. Taking part is voluntary. You can skip questions or stop before submitting. Please leave out names, service numbers and details that could identify you or someone else.

Your answers will be held by Lutheran Care in its approved systems. Only authorised staff who need them for this work will have access. Records are kept and securely disposed of under Lutheran Care's records requirements.

Information shared with the Department of Defence will be de-identified. We may also share information without consent where the law requires or permits it, including to protect someone's safety.

For questions, access or correction requests, or a privacy complaint, contact [feedback@lutherancare.org.au](mailto:feedback@lutherancare.org.au). See [Lutheran Care's Privacy Policy](https://www.lutherancare.org.au/privacy-policy/). If your answers cannot be linked back to you, we may not be able to retrieve or remove them after submission.

### Notes for the implementing agent

- Keep the above formal intended survey wording in the participant interface; put temporary collection-system status in the staff review material, as Jacob explicitly instructed.
- Do not write 'stored in Australia', 'Microsoft Forms', 'SharePoint', 'Tickit', or a specific number of years on the strength of these public sources. The sources simply do not establish these facts for this survey.
- A short voluntary/no-disadvantage statement is a sensible conduct rule for this consultation, but the verified LC public form only makes the explicit no-service-effect promise about complaints. Do not attribute a broader promise to that source.
- Do not promise absolute confidentiality. The privacy webpage's very brief wording is less precise than its linked June 2026 policy; follow the fuller current policy and the actual statutory reporting thresholds already audited.
- Parent/guardian permission plus separate child assent can follow LC's visible guardian-involvement practice while keeping names and signatures outside anonymous survey answers. Capacity/unsafe-parent cases should use the supervised pathway defined by the project's safeguarding procedure.
