# Interview request form

`contact.html` is the separate opt-in **Request an interview** form, with Lutheran Care identified in the page header. It invites people to share experiences and ideas to help plan Defence family support in Greater Darwin. A person contributing professionally uses the same self-request route as anyone else. The actual interviewer may be a member of the LC project team, including a supervised student; the team confirms who will attend when arranging the interview. It follows the consultation concept recovered from Thursday's ChatGPT task **讨论项目理解** (`6ab46d41-1b10-83ec-aab1-b6eeca5f3201`).

Its purpose is to arrange a service-consultation interview. It is not a service intake assessment, a counselling referral or a confirmed appointment. Completing the main survey is not a condition of using this form. The form does not receive survey answers, response IDs or other identifiers linking a request to an anonymous response.

**A genuine, contactable mobile number is mandatory on every route; email is optional and cannot replace the mobile.** The current interaction is one form followed by one review, with only self and guardian routes. It does not ask people to classify their ADF connection or professional role before arranging an appointment. The shorter introduction removes the survey-completion clause and three introductory explanation bullets. Interviewer details, the suggested adult duration of 30–40 minutes and actual arrangements are explained when arranging the appointment; interview consent is confirmed at its start.

Arranging a time is a brief message exchange through the selected channel, or a call if the person chooses it. There is no compulsory preliminary interview or separate confirmation call. Interview mode remains a useful, optional scheduling preference distinct from the channel used to agree a time.

The preview keeps the formal LC identity and shows the narrow notice **“Team review only. This form does not send your details.”** The final action is **Finish preview**, not a claim to submit. No receiver or real collection has been activated.

## Fields and interaction

| Field | Requirement and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Request for | Required: Me / My child under 18. Someone describing their own parenting or caring experience selects Me; the child option means the child will participate personally. Professional contributors also select Me. |
| Age group | Required: 18 or older / 15–17 / under 15 for a self-request; 15–17 / under 15 for a guardian request. Only a broad group is collected. A professional contributor uses the same self age choice. |
| Name to use | Required; up to 80 characters. A preferred name is sufficient. On the guardian route this is the parent or guardian’s own name, not the child’s name. |
| Mobile number | Required on every route, including professional, guardian and under-15 self-requests. Give a genuine mobile where the requester can be reached; the guardian supplies their own number. Format validation is not proof of ownership, reachability or safety. The receiving team establishes contact through the selected channel. |
| Email | Optional; up to 254 characters, validated when supplied. Never substitutes for the required mobile. It can be used for interview arrangements only as permitted; supplying it is not permission for marketing or unrelated follow-up. |
| Arranging a time | Required, with no default: call, text or email. The question asks how the team should arrange a time, not how to conduct a preliminary interview. Email becomes available only when a valid email has been entered. A required mobile does not authorise a call or text when email was chosen. No automatic fallback to another channel. |
| Voicemail | An unticked option appears only for calls. Permission is specifically to leave a voicemail saying Lutheran Care called. Changing the number or contact method clears this permission. |
| Contact instructions | Optional; up to 200 characters. Safe times to avoid and other contact instructions. Kept separate from general interview notes so the coordinator can find and follow them. |
| Interview mode | Optional: phone / video / in person / no preference. These are preferences for future scheduling, not an assertion that every mode is already staffed. Omitted on under-15 self-requests. |
| Interview notes | Optional; up to 600 characters, stored in `topic`: **Anything else we should know before the interview?** One short field can cover suitable times, practical arrangements or the topic the person wants to discuss. Professionals may mention their work here without completing a separate identity questionnaire. Save sensitive details for the interview and omit other people’s names, including identifiable client details. Omitted from under-15 self-requests and their review data. |
| Guardian authority | Required only for a guardian request and initially unticked: the requester declares parental responsibility or legal authority to make this request. It is a declaration for arranging contact, not verified proof or blanket interview consent. |
| Agreement | Required and initially unticked. Covers contact as selected and the visible information notice. The under-15 self-route uses a simpler agreement limited to contact details; it does not invite or obtain blanket agreement for sensitive information. |

No address, date of birth, rank, service number, unit, ADF-connection question, organisation/role question, document upload or survey-completion evidence is collected. There are no separate availability or participation-arrangement fields. One form contains the route, contact details, applicable optional preferences and information/agreement; review lets the person check and change details. The under-15 self-route omits interview mode and interview notes, retaining only the minimum contact and agreement route. The layout uses the survey’s Lutheran Care logo, typeface and colour treatment, with the information notice visible above agreement.

When text is selected, arrange the appointment by text; email works the same way. A chosen phone interview does not override the selected channel for arranging it. A call permission does not authorise an automatic SMS, and an email preference does not authorise a fallback call. No voicemail should be left unless the separate permission remains active. Follow the supplied instructions rather than infer safety from a valid number. Mobile reachability can be confirmed while making permitted arrangements; no automated verification, identity-document collection or extra confirmation call is implied.

## Children, young people and guardian requests

The form distinguishes **adulthood at 18** from the OAIC’s practical **capacity presumption at 15**. Section 4 of the [NT Age of Majority Act 1974](https://legislation.nt.gov.au/api/sitecore/Act/PDF?id=11583) sets full age at 18. [OAIC Chapter B, B.59–B.61](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-b-key-concepts) does not set a universal age of privacy consent: capacity depends on understanding. Fifteen is a permitted presumption where individual assessment is impracticable or unreasonable, unless circumstances indicate otherwise.

The earlier under-15 stop screen was an implementation choice, not a legal ban. It is replaced by the following routes:

| Route | Contact record and follow-up |
| --- | --- |
| Self, 18 or older | Own preferred name, mandatory safe mobile, optional email and contact permissions; interview mode and notes optional. Includes an adult speaking about parenting, caring or professional experiences. |
| Self, 15–17 | The same minimal fields. The page says a worker will explain the conversation and discuss permission or support before participation. |
| Self, under 15 | Own preferred name, mandatory safe mobile, optional email, contact permission and optional contact instructions only. No discussion-topic or other substantive preference fields, including in the whitelisted review data. The page explains that a worker will explain taking part and discuss permission first. |
| Parent or guardian, child under 18 | Parent or guardian’s own preferred name, mandatory mobile, optional email, contact permissions and authority declaration, plus the child’s broad age band. This arranges the child’s own participation. No child’s name, date of birth or private history is requested. |

The model uses `self_adult`, `self_youth`, `self_child`, `guardian_youth` and `guardian_child`. Professional participation does not create a separate route. An adult target is not valid on the child/guardian route. Changing the requester or age band clears personal fields, contact permissions, guardian declaration and consent, so adult or guardian details cannot silently become a child’s request. Guardian declarations are omitted from self-request review data. The under-15 self-route never serialises an old or injected topic or interview-mode value. The removed availability, participation-note, connection and organisation/role fields are not part of the request data.

The request is **not consent to an interview, recording, counselling or collection of further sensitive information**. When arranging a minor’s conversation, workers must establish understanding, appropriate permission and willingness before proceeding. A guardian checkbox records an assertion; workers verify authority if needed. Children can ask for help understanding the form. A self-request does not automatically notify a parent, especially where doing so could be unsafe. The programme needs a staff process for these decisions when collection is activated. See [the focused legal review](LEGAL_REVIEW.md) and [the child-participation review](CHILD_PARTICIPATION_REVIEW.md). The latter also explains the separate main questionnaire’s assistance and under-7 routes; those changes do not turn a contact request into interview consent.

## Professional contributions and small-team operation

Professional contributors select Me and their age group, then use the shared mandatory-mobile contact and scheduling flow. They can mention their work in the optional interview notes. At the interview, the team confirms whether they are speaking about professional experience, their own family experience or both and keeps those perspectives distinguishable. The separate professional invitation and discussion guide remain available to the team; no separate public registration branch is needed. A self-nomination is not evidence of authority to speak officially for an organisation.

[INTERVIEW_TEAM_GUIDE.md](INTERVIEW_TEAM_GUIDE.md) supplies the lightweight operating plan, blank team/slot arrangements, family and professional interview prompts, private confirmation/cancellation templates, finite follow-up, a minimal contact-log schema and handover. Use one coordinator and backup, grouped private appointment slots and phone/video preferences. Assign interview duties by competence and authorised role; do not assume that Defence family team members are either untrained or available as full-time interviewers. Participants can request someone they do not know.

Before collection opens, LC fills in its actual people, slots, receiving system, achievable reply window and end-of-round arrangements. Those items are part of the proposed operating arrangement, not information Jacob must supply to finish this preview. No date or response deadline is invented here. Request handling is separate from the survey’s response-review process. The support resource remains available whether or not a person requests an interview or obtains an appointment.

## Free resource

The same free digital support finder is offered beneath the contact form and review screen, and on the finish screen. The button reads **Find support in a few clicks** and the description identifies the free guide for Defence members and families, without implying the visitor has already completed the survey. The navigator remains NT-wide while this consultation covers Greater Darwin, including Litchfield. It is available without providing contact details or agreeing to a conversation. `thank-you-resource.js` now points to the exact first-party `support.html` page. The link also accepts a public HTTPS URL without embedded credentials for future resources; other relative URLs remain invalid. It opens in a new tab with `noopener noreferrer` and `referrerpolicy="no-referrer"`. No answers, contact details or participant identifiers are added to the destination.

## Information notice and sources

The participant notice identifies Lutheran Care, explains that details are for arranging the consultation, describes intended custody and access by authorised project-team members, separates contact details from survey answers and reports to Defence, and provides ways to update details, cancel contact, seek access or raise a concern. It does not repeat the anonymous survey's inability-to-withdraw wording: a contact request is identifiable and can be located by its contact details. Cancelling contact does not promise immediate deletion of records that Lutheran Care must retain. Team membership alone does not require access to all named requests.

## Current implementation and real collection

This is a formal LC review build with the explicit, unobtrusive notice **“Team review only. This form does not send your details.”** `contact-model.mjs` owns the field limits, validation and notice; `contact.js` holds entered details only in page memory. Optional email is a form field, not a sending mechanism. There is no receiving endpoint, storage API, local/session storage, answer download or email submission. `contact.html` sets `connect-src 'none'` and `form-action 'none'`; external privacy links use a no-referrer policy. Leaving the page clears the in-memory request and rendered details, including when returning through the browser's back/forward cache.

The review ends with **Finish preview** and a completion explanation that the details were not sent, alongside the shared resource and options to review or clear details. It does not claim receipt, an appointment or a promised callback. Use invented details for team walkthroughs. Public hosting can still receive ordinary page-request metadata; this implementation's claim is that entered form contents are not transmitted to a receiver.

Before replacing this review flow with real collection, the implementation and notice need to describe the actual Lutheran Care receiving record, authorised access, provider and any overseas processing, and applicable retention arrangements. Those facts cannot be inferred from the organisation-wide policy or supplied by a generic disclaimer. The receiving process must also preserve contact permissions, the separation from anonymous survey responses and staff decisions about minors’ capacity, permission and willingness. This is the remaining operational work for activation, not a claim that the prototype already collects information or guarantees legal compliance.
