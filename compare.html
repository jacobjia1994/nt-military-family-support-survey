Defence Family Support ProgramGreater Darwin

Review two survey approaches

Both questionnaires ask about the same Greater Darwin Defence family issues. Please consider which one makes it easier for people to describe their experience and gives the team useful information for planning support.

Detailed choices

People select relevant issues, kinds of help, sources and outcomes, with optional space to explain each priority need.

  • Twenty issue areas with specific examples
  • Structured follow-up for up to two areas
  • Optional written explanation for each need
Open detailed-choice survey

Open responses

People use the same issue list, then describe the help they needed, what they tried and what remains missing in their own words.

  • The same twenty issue areas and examples
  • Written follow-up for up to two areas
  • Separate optional answers for each priority need
Open written-response survey
