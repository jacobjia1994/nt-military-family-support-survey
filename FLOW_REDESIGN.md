# Consultation flow and measurement contract

Updated 28 September 2026. The adult and 8–17 questionnaire uses schema `8.1`, revision `2026-09-28-issue-cued-accounts`. The separate guardian-supported form for children aged 7 or younger remains schema `1.3`. This public page is a **team-review preview**: the pre-consent notice and finish screen explain that answers are not sent to Lutheran Care or saved by the page. There is no response receiver. Lutheran Care and its IT team still need to choose and verify collection, access and storage before fieldwork. See [COLLECTION_HANDOFF.md](COLLECTION_HANDOFF.md).

## Purpose and route

The written survey is the main broad feedback channel for the Greater Darwin Defence Family Support consultation. One initial selectable issue page is a memory prompt and route. **Every selected issue leads to one question page**, with an opening prompt fitted to that issue. Adults see 17 issues; 8–17-year-olds see eight. A selected issue can be left without a substantive answer. Within an issue page, a respondent-led entry may describe an experience, a future idea or a neutral account without choosing either label, and the person may add further entries under that issue. A connected posting, schooling and service-navigation story may be told once; analysts can apply several thematic codes later. The old `7.2` recent/future domain loops and generic participation-format questions remain superseded. Neither their values nor schema `8.0` free-entry records are silently mapped into schema `8.1`.

1. The invitation retains adult, 8–17 and 7-or-younger paths, inline information, permission and assent rules. The preview limitation is visible before consent. Adult and youth background asks ADF relationship and optional broad residential area/suburb; adults may add age band and residence duration. There is no separate open community-connection essay before the issues.
2. The next substantive step asks which areas the person would like to talk about. The grouped 17 adult or eight youth issues include an **Other issue** with optional write-in. This wording welcomes positive experiences, difficulties and future ideas. The selection does not assert that a problem occurred or help was needed. The person can leave the list unanswered and continue; a selected-but-blank issue remains a selection, not an account.
3. The survey opens exactly one question page for each selected issue. It uses that issue's own example and story/future wording. On a topic page, the respondent may choose **an experience** or **something coming up or an idea**, or write a neutral substantive answer without making that choice; they may add another entry for the same issue or move on without detail. A later topic page may be skipped if the same connected story was already described. An explicit experience is framed by time living in Greater Darwin during the past 12 months for adults or three months for youth, or since arrival if shorter. A future idea does not require a recent difficulty. Do not infer a past or future classification from an unanswered type choice.
4. Each entry asks one optional narrative. Where appropriate, experiences also show an optional support-status choice about **useful help for that situation**, separating enough, some, none despite trying, none when help was needed but not sought, and no help needed. Adult safety and bereavement pages omit this status and the extra helped/difficult boxes; their absence must be recorded as **not asked**. One distinct optional box asks what would be useful to keep, change or add. These are the normal path; respondents need not complete a chain of detailed questions.
5. On an experience, **Add what helped or was difficult** optionally opens those two extra boxes before the useful-change question. **Give me a prompt to explain my idea** optionally shows a response-type choice and changes the label of the *same* useful-change box. A described help or activity can make an optional practical-details control available even without that wording helper. For a future idea, the first narrative can supply the proposal when the second box is blank. Only an eligible described offer **and explicit opt-in** shows one practical question tied to that entry. A mixed idea may specify its practical component in that answer; a process-only change does not trigger attendance, timing or venue questions. No question assumes Lutheran Care's Berrimah office is an activity venue.
6. Topic pages and review allow entries to be edited or removed while preserving the other entries. A zero-account route has one optional final note. With two or more accounts containing described changes, one optional free-text priority question appears after the issue pages; there is no 100-item ranking menu or repeated general “anything else” prompt.
7. Review lists compact summaries with the issue and an Edit action. **Finish preview** leads to a screen saying answers were not sent or saved. The separate interview request and support finder remain independent optional links without answer or identifier transfer.

The optional broad-area answer controls consultation routing. Darwin, Palmerston, Litchfield or another Greater Darwin area reaches the main questionnaire; undisclosed area also continues without being labelled confirmed local residence. Outside Greater Darwin with previous local residence has a separate historical account route; explicitly never living locally leads to the scope explanation. Service location does not substitute for residence. Area-only answers remain separate from named suburbs; Palmerston does not imply Palmerston City.

## Repeat capacity and answer preservation

`answers.issue_cues` stores issue IDs selected in the initial list, in display order. `answers.issue_other` stores the optional Other issue label. These are routing/prompt answers, not accounts or unmet-need flags. `answers.accounts` is an ordered array of **0 to 100 active accounts across the whole response**. An account created under an issue page has a `topic_id`; a voluntary account added from the general manage/start path may be uncategorised and has no `topic_id`. Do not impute a selected topic to an uncategorised account. The ceiling is technical capacity, never a target or progress goal. The interface counts entries actually added; it does not show “of 100” progress. At the ceiling, adding another is prevented with a respectful explanation that existing entries remain available for review, editing or removal. No account may be silently overwritten or truncated. Removing one releases a slot. A replacement receives a fresh ID, while surviving account IDs and text stay unchanged. An empty draft does not consume a slot. Unselecting an issue preserves written entries already tagged to that issue; they remain reviewable and are not silently deleted.

Each account stores its stable `id`, optional `topic_id`, `kind` (`experience`, `future` or `unspecified`), optional `story`, `useful_change`, and applicable optional branch fields. `unspecified` preserves a substantive answer given without choosing experience/future; it must not acquire a past recall marker or past-help question by inference. `help_status` belongs only to eligible experiences, not adult safety or bereavement pages. `helped` and `difficult` require explicit detail expansion and are likewise absent from those sensitive pages. `proposal_type` is retained when the wording helper or practical opt-in was used. `practical_detail` requires an eligible type, a described change in `useful_change` or a future account's `story`, and explicit `practical_opt_in`. The `other` proposal type can describe the practical component in that same free-text answer; a process-only idea is ineligible. Hidden or inapplicable values are excluded from the cleaned export; blank, skipped, explicit negative, unsure and declined answers remain distinct. Internal draft and next-ID state is never exported.

The export carries schema/revision, questionnaire version, consultation route, residence precision, local recall context, respondent-perspective analysis unit, `max_accounts: 100`, and `collection_mode: internal_review_no_transmission`. The separate under-7 form has its own child-expression and guardian-observation records; it does not inherit the account sequence. Contact requests remain separate, with no response/contact join key.

## Analysis and use

Count response records, initial selected issues and substantive account entries separately. One response with 100 accounts remains one response, not 100 people or families. A selected issue with no account is recorded as selected without detail; an unselected issue is unknown rather than a negative finding. A respondent-level theme is counted once for that response even if several accounts mention it; account-level summaries may separately count situations with a clear label. Keep survey cue selections, volunteered interview statements, prompted elaborations and analyst inferences distinguishable. Do not pool unlike raw counts or treat volunteer responses as Greater Darwin prevalence. Apply [ANALYSIS_PROTOCOL.md](ANALYSIS_PROTOCOL.md) to every chart, report and export.

The interview guide uses the same situation, support, useful-change and conditional practical constructs with selective oral probes. Targeted conversations address a decision-changing why/how, an apparent mismatch, a missing perspective or someone unable to contribute comfortably in writing. Cognitive/usability pretests test the instrument and are kept separate from substantive findings. See [INTERVIEW_TEAM_GUIDE.md](INTERVIEW_TEAM_GUIDE.md).
