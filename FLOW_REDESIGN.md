# RAND Appendix A adult flow and measurement contract

Current adult presentation: `rand-appendix-a-greater-darwin/1`. The [source crosswalk](RAND_ITEM_CROSSWALK.md) covers original Q1–Q67. The sample questionnaire's question language, nine problem pages, answer lists, two-stage prioritisation, resource matrices and closing are retained wherever local facts allow. The prior 9×5 cue-led schema 9.0 is historical.

1. **Welcome and participation.** The adult Page 1 identifies eligible service members and adult family members connected with Greater Darwin; Page 2 follows RAND's purpose/participation/consent structure without US survey licence, legal authority, confidentiality promises or an untested completion time. The child/young-person link leaves the adult instrument.
2. **Study and context (Q1–Q11).** Preserve recruitment and prior-survey questions, Service/status/partner/dependant/deployment/base items and their original conditional referents. Three local helpers clarify current partner, independent child and Greater Darwin connection without taking a RAND Q number.
3. **Problem recognition (Q12–Q21).** Present nine original RAND domain pages with 95 source-derived options, including one mutually exclusive explicit no-problem option in each. Q21 remains the original Other text opportunity. Blank, explicit none and inapplicable remain distinct. No problem reported bypasses detailed needs/resources and proceeds to Page 8 background.
4. **Most significant problems and help (Q22–Q25).** When more than two problem categories were reported, Q22 displays only those categories and their checked specifics, inviting two most significant. One or two categories carry forward without a false explicit ranking. Q23/Q24 repeat RAND's ten help types for selected categories, including no assistance needed. Q25 selects the two most significant help types only when more than two were reported for a category. Never choose the first two silently after a skipped question.
5. **Resources and outcomes (Q26–Q36).** Up to four problem×need pairs receive Q26–Q29 contact questions. Q30–Q33 show all seven original characteristics for contacted/noncontacted military and nonmilitary resource categories; Q34 separately covers personal networks. Q35 repeats its original five-point helpfulness matrix for each contacted resource within a specific pair. Q36 retains the nine-row, four-choice hypothetical loss matrix. No new all-sources unmet-need measure is attributed to RAND.
6. **Background and attitudes (Q37–Q67).** Keep applicable rank, service duration, sex/age/education, deployment, caring, child support, housing, work/study and posting context. Q49/Q50 use Australian Indigenous-status and ancestry constructs rather than treating US Hispanic/race categories as equivalent. Q62–Q66 preserve the original member/spouse service-attitude questions; Q67 remains open for further comments. The final page thanks the person and offers verified Australian support contacts.

All respondent-visible wording is actual-survey style, including **Confirm and submit** and the neutral thank-you. The current static site nevertheless has no response receiver or persistence; this is an internal operating fact, not a displayed status. Do not claim an actual response was received or treat this site as a live collection channel. Before real collection, LC must approve participant information and answer custody and prove a durable submission/export.
