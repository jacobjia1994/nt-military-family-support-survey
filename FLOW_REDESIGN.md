# Consultation flow and measurement contract

Updated 28 September 2026. The adult and 8–17 questionnaire uses schema `8.0`, revision `2026-09-28-respondent-accounts`. The separate guardian-supported form for children aged 7 or younger remains schema `1.3`. This is a **team-review preview**: answers stay in page memory and there is no response receiver. Lutheran Care and its IT team still need to choose and verify collection, access and storage before fieldwork. See [COLLECTION_HANDOFF.md](COLLECTION_HANDOFF.md).

## Purpose and route

The written survey is the main broad feedback channel for the Greater Darwin Defence Family Support consultation. One respondent-chosen experience or future idea is the unit of follow-up. A connected posting, schooling and service-navigation story remains one account unless the respondent chooses to add another; analysts can apply several topic tags later. The old adult/youth `7.2` recent/future 17-domain loops and global participation questions are superseded. Their field values must not be silently mapped into schema `8.0`.

1. The invitation page retains the adult, 8–17 and 7-or-younger paths, inline information, permission and assent rules. Adult and youth background asks ADF relationship and optional broad residential area/suburb; adults may add age band and residence duration. The v8 main route has no separate open community-connection essay before accounts.
2. An adult or young person may choose **an experience** or **something coming up or an idea**, add another after an account, or continue with no account. An experience is framed by time living in Greater Darwin during the past 12 months for adults or three months for youth, or since arrival if shorter. A future idea does not require a recent difficulty.
3. Each account asks one optional narrative. Experiences alone also show one optional support-status choice about **useful help for that situation**, separating enough, some, none despite trying, none when help was needed but not sought, and no help needed. This wording works for a family or child's situation without requiring the adult to call it their own. One distinct optional box asks what would be useful to keep, change or add. These are the normal path; respondents need not complete a chain of detailed questions.
4. On an experience, **Add what helped or was difficult** optionally opens those two extra boxes before the useful-change question. **Give me a prompt to explain my idea** optionally shows a response-type choice and changes the label of the *same* useful-change box. It does not create a second required explanation.
5. A described change makes an optional practical-details control discoverable even when the wording helper was not opened. For a future idea, the first narrative can provide that description when the second box is blank; the person need not repeat it. The respondent then identifies information help, a conversation, an activity, or the relevant component of a mixed idea they might use. Only an eligible described offer **and explicit opt-in** shows one practical question tied to that account. A mixed idea uses that free-text answer to say which part needs practical arrangements. A process-only change does not trigger attendance, timing or venue questions. No question assumes Lutheran Care's Berrimah office is an activity venue.
6. A manage page lets the person add, edit or remove accounts. A zero-account route has one optional final note. A one-account route goes from manage to review. With two or more accounts containing described changes, one optional free-text priority question appears; there is no 100-item ranking menu or repeated general “anything else” prompt.
7. Review lists compact account summaries with a separate Edit action. **Finish preview** leads to a screen saying answers were not sent or saved. The separate interview request and support finder remain independent optional links without answer or identifier transfer.

The optional broad-area answer controls consultation routing. Darwin, Palmerston, Litchfield or another Greater Darwin area reaches the main questionnaire; undisclosed area also continues without being labelled confirmed local residence. Outside Greater Darwin with previous local residence has a separate historical account route; explicitly never living locally leads to the scope explanation. Service location does not substitute for residence. Area-only answers remain separate from named suburbs; Palmerston does not imply Palmerston City.

## Repeat capacity and answer preservation

`answers.accounts` is an ordered array of **0 to 100 active accounts**. The ceiling is technical capacity, never a target or progress goal. The interface counts entries actually added; it does not show “of 100” progress. At the ceiling, adding another is prevented with a respectful explanation that existing entries remain available for review, editing or removal. No account may be silently overwritten or truncated. Removing one releases a slot. A replacement receives a fresh ID, while surviving account IDs and text stay unchanged. An empty draft left on Back or Continue is discarded and does not consume a slot.

Each account stores its stable `id`, `kind` (`experience` or `future`), optional `story`, `useful_change`, and the applicable optional branch fields. `help_status` belongs only to experiences. `helped` and `difficult` require the explicit detail expansion. `proposal_type` is retained when the wording helper or practical opt-in was used. `practical_detail` requires an eligible type, a described change in `useful_change` or a future account's `story`, and explicit `practical_opt_in`. The `other` type can describe the practical component in that same free-text answer; `process` alone is ineligible. Hidden or inapplicable values are excluded from the cleaned export; blank, skipped, explicit negative, unsure and declined answers remain distinct. The internal next-ID counter is never exported.

The export carries schema/revision, questionnaire version, consultation route, residence precision, local recall context, respondent-perspective analysis unit, `max_accounts: 100`, and `collection_mode: internal_review_no_transmission`. The separate under-7 form has its own child-expression and guardian-observation records; it does not inherit the account sequence. Contact requests remain separate, with no response/contact join key.

## Analysis and use

Count response records and account entries separately. One response with 100 accounts remains one response, not 100 people or families. A respondent-level theme is counted once for that response even if several accounts mention it; account-level summaries may separately count situations with a clear label. Keep survey selections, volunteered interview statements, prompted elaborations and analyst inferences distinguishable. Do not pool unlike raw counts or treat volunteer responses as Greater Darwin prevalence. Apply [ANALYSIS_PROTOCOL.md](ANALYSIS_PROTOCOL.md) to every chart, report and export.

The interview guide uses the same situation, support, useful-change and conditional practical constructs with selective oral probes. Targeted conversations address a decision-changing why/how, an apparent mismatch, a missing perspective or someone unable to contribute comfortably in writing. Cognitive/usability pretests test the instrument and are kept separate from substantive findings. See [INTERVIEW_TEAM_GUIDE.md](INTERVIEW_TEAM_GUIDE.md).
